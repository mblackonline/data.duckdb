About, Data Use & Acceptable Use

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This proof of concept explores CARTA's public transportation data through reports, filters, and an AI assistant. Public reports require no sign-in. AI features require Microsoft sign-in and explicit authorization.

Public reports and downloads

Public report queries run in your browser using DuckDB and retrieve data from public sources. CSV exports are downloaded to your device. Data sources and hosting services process the network requests needed to provide this service.

Microsoft sign-in and cookies

Microsoft handles authentication. This application does not receive your Microsoft password. It temporarily holds account information, including your display name and authentication tokens, in browser memory. It sends an access token to its backend to verify your identity and AI permissions.

Microsoft uses cookies on its sign-in domains to manage authentication and sign-in sessions. Whether a session continues after you close your browser depends on your sign-in choices, browser settings, and organizational policies.

Microsoft Entra stores account or guest-account information, access assignments, and consent records. Its sign-in logs can include your account identifier, sign-in time, application, outcome, IP address, approximate location, and browser or device information. Appropriately authorized tenant administrators can access these records. Retention depends on the tenant's licensing and logging configuration. Read Microsoft's information about sign-in cookies, sign-in logs, and log retention.

AI requests and Groq

AI requests pass through this site's backend to Groq, an external AI provider. The backend uses the operator's server-held Groq API key, and usage is attributed to the operator's Groq account. Your Microsoft password, sign-in token, and account identifier are not sent to Groq for authentication or added to the AI prompt by the application.

Groq receives your question, selected table schemas, public dataset sample values, and other available table names. If a query needs correction, database error text is also sent and can include SQL or data values. Information you include in your question is shared with Groq. Generated SQL runs in your browser.

Do not submit passwords, API keys, personal information, confidential material, or other sensitive information to the AI assistant.

Application storage and provider retention

This version of the application does not create or update persistent user profiles, user hashes, per-user quota counters, report-correction records, or AI activity history. Account information, tokens, questions, and responses are processed temporarily to provide the current session and requests. The application does not save them in a backend database or application logs.

There are no application-enforced per-user daily or minute quotas. The browser attempts one automatic SQL correction if a generated query fails. Provider and hosting limits still apply. Any usage records created by an earlier version remain until the deployment operator removes them.

Netlify provides hosting and backend processing. Provider operational records are governed by their own policies and settings. Groq's handling of AI inputs and outputs depends on its policies and the operator's data-retention settings. See Groq's data-handling information.

Report limitations and acceptable use

This service is experimental. Source data can be incomplete or delayed, and AI-generated reports can contain errors. Verify results before relying on them. Refer to the original data sources for licensing and reuse conditions.

Use the service lawfully. Do not bypass access controls or provider limits, disrupt the service, or submit information you are not authorized to share. Access can be restricted for misuse. Features and availability can change as the proof of concept develops.

Contact

For access questions, privacy concerns, or problem reports, contact the person who provided you with this proof of concept or your organization's deployment administrator.
